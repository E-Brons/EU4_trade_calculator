## Verified facts from response_1 (ground truth from 82 saves; do not contradict, build on them)

- `power_fraction = trunc3((val - t_out + t_in) / node.retain_power)` for collecting entries (42,948 entries, 7 failures, all S64 `genua`); `potential = trunc3((t_out - t_in) / node.total)` for entries with transfers (3,584 of 3,584); `pull_power` rule R03 holds in 5,815 of 5,819 nodes.
- `max_demand` exists at every country at every node (3,426,880 of 3,427,230 entries), 0.281..2.793, below 1.0 in 308,713 entries, and `val = trunc3(max_pow * max_demand)` in 85,892 of 85,892 entries.
- `num_collectors_including_pirates = num_collectors + 1` in 5,906 of 5,906 nodes; every node holds a `PIR` entry with only `max_demand=1.0`; `collector_power_including_pirates == collector_power` everywhere.
- Node `p_pow` = sum of stored province `trade_power` including rebel-held provinces (6,350 of 6,504); `max = p_pow + sum(max_pow - prev - province_power)`, i.e. `p_pow` plus ship power and flat extras (6,503 of 6,504); `highest_power` = largest single province `trade_power` (6,480 of 6,504); `collector_power = retain_power`; `top_power_values` = effective powers `val - t_out + t_in` (6,525 of 6,525); `top_provinces_values` = entries' `province_power`.
- `already_sent = province_power / 5 * k`, k whole number 1..5 (17,108 of 17,114 entries); k equals the node's number of upstream nodes in 89.5% of entries.
- `trade_goods_size` is a 33-entry list in the key order of the top-level `change_price` block; `local_value = sum(size_i * current_price_i) / 12` (R13 response_1 C-01).
- `most_recent_treasure_ship_passage` is `1.1.1` in all 6,560 node blocks.
- Country block: `merchants={envoy={...}}` has `type=1` in all 107,275 envoys; `action=2` in 63,648, absent in 43,619, `action=1` in 8; #envoys with `action=2` = #nodes with `has_trader` in 42,588 of 42,754 country-saves.
- Update 2026-10-05, saves U03 (1691.1.9), U04 (in-game date 1691.11.1; file name says 11.17) and U05 (1693.4.15) of the S79/S80 campaign (single script runs, not independently re-computed): `val = trunc3(max_pow x max_demand)` 866/866, 869/869, 859/859; `top_power_values = val - t_out + t_in` for every pair with an entry in all three; `max = p_pow + sum(max_pow - prev - province_power)` 80/80, 80/80, 78/80; the two exceptions in U05 (`ethiopia`, `gulf_of_aden`) are nodes where a country that lost its last province (AFA) has no entry any more but `top_power` still lists it (2.61 and 7.518), `max` exceeds `p_pow` + entries by 2.0 and 7.0, `pull_power`/`retain_power` exceed the calculation by 2.610 and 7.518 and the link `ethiopia -> gulf_of_aden` keeps `incoming.add` 0.377 (AFA's old `add` 0.065 x 5.804): these node and link fields can be one monthly computation older than the per-country entries (inferred from one event). Stage `raw_power` fails in 527/883, 528/886, 519/873 entries, as in S79/S80; the error (predicted minus recorded) is -2, -7, -15, -17, -20 or -22 (second-pass correction: it is not a constant 22.0; 22 is the largest).
- Update 2026-10-05, Venice series (24 saves U07-U30 of one game, VEN from 1444.11.11 to 1445.07.02; all three merchants recalled between U25 1445.3.1 and U26 1445.3.31, then sent back one per month; see response_1 'Update 2026-10-05 - Venice series'): (a) `transfer_home_bonus` = 0.1 x the number of the country's steering merchants (node entries with `type` and `has_trader`, any link of any node), and 0.0 when the country has at least one merchant collecting away from its home node; 665 of 665 countries with a home entry in U30 and in U27 (raw-text second pass), 869 of 870 in the TUR played saves; VEN 0.3 / 0.0 (U26, mid-month) / 0.0 / 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.3; it is refreshed at once by a merchant action and is 0.0 for all 1,380 countries in the save written before the first day passed (U07). ## Points to answer

**Q1 (closes request_1 Q4 and Q6, node part) - Parser-project definitions of the node-level trade fields.** Missing: an external source for each node and entry field, with path + line + version. Fields: `local_value`, `trade_goods_size`, `current`, `outgoing`, `value_added_outgoing`, `retention`, `retain_power`, `pull_power`, `total` (node and entry), `p_pow`, `max`, `highest_power`, `collector_power`, `collector_power_including_pirates`, `num_collectors(_including_pirates)`, `top_power(_values)`, `top_provinces(_values)`, `most_recent_treasure_ship_passage`, `already_sent`, `potential`, `power_fraction`, `max_pow`, `max_demand`, `prev`, `t_in`, `t_out`, `t_from`, `t_to`, `type`, `steer_power`, `add`, `modifier`. Evidence: our own definitions above come from data only. Expected form: a table `field | source file:line | version | the source's own wording (verbatim) | agrees / disagrees with the verified fact above`. Mark fields that no parser documents as `UNKNOWN`; do not infer meanings from field names. If a source disagrees with a verified fact, list it as rejected and say which source. Update 2026-10-05: also say which node fields (`top_power`, `top_power_values`, `max`, `pull_power`, `retain_power`, link `incoming.add`) are refreshed when a country loses its last province before the next monthly tick and which keep the previous monthly values (U05 `ethiopia`, `gulf_of_aden`, see Verified facts).

**Q2 (closes request_1 Q4) - Country-level fields.** Missing: meaning, unit and source for `trade_embargoes`, `trade_embargoed_by`, `num_of_trade_embargos`, `transfer_trade_power_from`, `transfer_trade_power_to`, `traded`, `traded_bonus`, `trade_mission`, `num_ships_protecting_trade`, `mercantilism`, `trade_port`, and inside `merchants={envoy={...}}` the keys `type` and `action`. Evidence (response_1 Q4 table): `traded` is 33 numbers in `change_price` goods order for all 113,160 country-saves, its sum equals none of `money`, `val`, node `current` (136 / 0 / 22 of 42,754); `traded_bonus` is a list of goods indices in 405 country-saves (S01 MAJ `[31]`, S80 TUR `[16, 18, 26, 27, 31]`); `trade_mission` is a float 0.013..2.0 present in the four played-save files (418 country-saves) and, in the Venice series, from the first tick on (193-194 countries, VEN 0.214 constant; values a/b such as 0.142, 0.25, 0.666, 1.142, 0.166, 0.285, 0.307, 0.333); envoy `action=1` occurs 8 times, `action=2` means placed in a node, absent means unplaced (inferred); `num_ships_protecting_trade` equals the light ships on protect-mission fleets in 143 of 145 country-saves and differs for S79 TUR (98 vs 100 on missions). Expected form: per field `meaning | unit | source (file:line, version) | quote | confidence`, then one line: does the field feed a trade calculation (yes/no/UNKNOWN) and why.

**Q3 (closes request_1 Q1, `max_demand`) - A sourced definition of `max_demand`.** Missing: which modifiers the game folds into it. Evidence: it exists for every country at every node, varies by node for one country (TUR 2.11 at some nodes, 1.895 at its home node, 2.110 abroad in S79), is below 1.0 in 308,713 entries, and the draft's `1.0 + global_trade_power_modifiers` is contradicted by those entries. Expected form: one claim with quote stating what the field is in game terms (the per-country trade-power multiplier at a node?) and which effects (global trade power, away-from-capital penalty, embargo, home-node bonus, domestic/foreign) are inside it, each with source and version; `UNKNOWN` for every effect without a quote. Do not re-derive the R01/R02 numbers; those have their own requests.

**Q4 (closes request_1 Q1, pirates) - `PIR` entry and `num_collectors_including_pirates`.** Missing: what the game means by the extra collector. Evidence: a `PIR={ max_demand=1.0 }` entry exists in all 6,560 node blocks (35 of them, the `cape_of_good_hope` nodes, also carry `potential=-0.001` on `PIR`), `num_collectors_including_pirates` is always `num_collectors + 1`, and the power fields are identical with and without pirates. Expected form: a sourced statement of what `PIR` is in the data (a pseudo-country? privateer power?), when its power is non-zero, and whether the `+1` is constant by design; `UNKNOWN` otherwise. **Q5 (closes request_1 Q2, `already_sent`) - The rule behind `already_sent`.** Missing: the meaning and the rule that fixes the whole number `k`. Evidence: `already_sent = province_power / 5 * k`, k = 2: 5,749; 1: 5,269; 3: 3,286; 4: 1,814; 5: 990 (6 entries non-integer: 1.04, 1.11, 4.2); S14 `kongo` KON `already_sent=3.954` with `province_power=19.77` (k=1); S14 `zambezi` ZIM `5.808` vs `29.04` (k=1); S14 `california` CNK `6.82` vs `17.05` (k=2); `k` equals the number of upstream nodes of the node in 15,316 of 17,114 entries (89.5%), exception S01 `mexico` (in-degree 2, k=1); the field is absent on 36,828 entries with `province_power > 0` (1,228 of them with `province_power >= 10`). Expected form: does any source describe a field with this behaviour (same `/5` as `prev`, counted per upstream link)? Give the source and the rule for when it is present/absent, or `UNKNOWN`.

**Q6 (closes request_1 Q2, `retention`; also the only failing row of response_1 Q5) - Is there a source for the exact `retention` and `current` arithmetic?** Missing: anything that explains why `retention` is not `trunc3(retain/(retain+pull))`. Evidence: S14 `alexandria`: `retain_power=159.516`, `pull_power=101.429`, quotient 0.61133, stored `retention=0.612`, `current=5.052` (stored retention times `local_value + sum(incoming)` = 8.254 x 0.612 = 5.0514 reproduces `current`; with 0.6113 it would be 5.0457). The README log already says `retention` is not a plain truncation. Expected form: a sourced formula or constant (a floor, a mixing term, a fixed-point step size such as 1/1024, a rounding to 3 decimals of some other quantity) that gives 0.612 here; or `UNKNOWN`. If you propose a candidate, test it on the row above and show the arithmetic; do not fit it.

**Q7 (closes request_1 Q2, `potential`) - `potential` on nodes with no power.** Missing: the meaning of the negative constants. Evidence: in `cape_of_good_hope` of 35 saves (no `total`, no `p_pow`, `retention=1.0`) every stub entry carries `potential=-0.002` (23,625 entries) and `PIR` carries `-0.001` (35 entries); no other node carries `potential` on an entry without transfers. Expected form: source or `UNKNOWN`; if you can explain -0.002 and -0.001 from a formula with a zero denominator, show it.

**Q8 (narrowed by the Venice series) - `transfer_home_bonus`: source and limits.** The rule is verified from the saves (Verified facts (a), (b)): 0.1 per steering merchant, 0.0 with any away collector, added to `max_demand` at the home node at the tick. Missing: (1) a source for the field name and the rule (game text, parser documentation) and its relation to the define `TRADE_POWER_HOME_BONUS` or any other define; (2) whether a cap exists (0.9 is the largest value in the earlier corpus; the Venice saves reach 0.3) and whether a merchant in transit (sent, not yet arrived) counts; (3) why the bonus is zero with an away collector. Form: a source + quote for each, or `UNKNOWN`.

**Q9 (closes request_1 Q6) - Version banners and provenance.** Missing: for each cited source the game version it refers to and the direct URL/path; and for the three draft claims C-01..C-03 either an outside source or an explicit statement that they are project-verified only. Expected form: a list `claim | source | version | reliability (one line)`.
