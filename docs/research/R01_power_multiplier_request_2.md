## Verified facts from response_1 (ground truth; do not contradict, do not re-derive)

- V1. Corpus: 82 saves, 3,420,320 `(country,node)` `max_demand` values; `max_demand` exists for every country at every node. `val = max_pow * max_demand`.
- V2. For a country without `trade_embargoed_by`, outside its home node, its top-province nodes and its away-collecting nodes, `max_demand` is the same number at every node: 42,632 of 42,636 (save,country) groups (99.99%) have exactly one value (the 4 others: SUN and DLI in S80/U01). So no node property (goods, node size, trade company region) is involved for ordinary countries.
- V3. Classes: *domestic* = the node of `country.trade_port` OR a node whose `top_provinces[0]` is the tag; everything else is *foreign*. Top-node value equals home-node value in 839 of 873 cases (96%); differs from both in 16 (colonial nations C05/C08/C10 and BNG in each of the four played-save files; these are the home-bonus cases of V8). Home vs foreign value (non-embargoed, 42,636 groups): home lower 20,233, equal 20,569, higher 1,834; ratio home/foreign min 0.528, median 1.0, max 4.253.
- V4. The foreign value is one scalar per country-save with 749 distinct values. Most common: 1.009 (13,786 country-saves), 1.036, 0.993, 1.007, 1.031, 1.339, 1.026, 1.012 (these 8 = 59.7%). Min 0.281 (ZUN, S42), max 2.166. Below 1.0 in 3,815 country-saves (207 tags). Exactly 1.000: once (S28 ETH). Correlation with prestige -0.09, power projection 0.17, mercantilism 0.09, government rank 0.08 (R² <= 0.03): no single one of these four explains it.
- V5. TUR (S79, 1665.4.22): foreign 2.110, domestic 1.895 (clean rows with no embargoer own power). GEN (S79): foreign 1.907, no clean domestic row (genua 1.241 is embargoed by SWI, PAP, LAN).
- V6. Embargo acts inside `max_demand`: embargoed-country rows (9,324): no embargoer with own power -> reduced 2 / not reduced 7,968; some embargoer with own power -> reduced 1,206 / not reduced 148 (reduced = more than 0.3% below the class cap; 1,284 / 70 with an absolute 0.0015 threshold) (120 of the 148 have embargoer share < 1% of the node's own power). *Own power* = `max_pow - prev` (province + ship + flat merchant/capital extras, NOT propagated). With province+ship only, 320 reduced rows have no embargoer power: the embargoer's flat merchant power counts.
- V7. Steering and merchant-without-action do not change `max_demand`; collecting away halves it (R02).
- V8. Home-node bonus: in S79, S80, U01, U02 (2 saves; S79 = U02 and S80 = U01 are copies, independence of S79 and S80 not established), for non-embargoed countries with >= 1 steering merchant and nobody collecting away, home - top = exactly 0.1 x number of steering merchants (16 of 16; e.g. C05 8 merchants +0.8, BNG +0.3/+0.4); with a merchant collecting away home == top (12 of 12). In the 78 start snapshots 831 such countries show home == top (no bonus).
