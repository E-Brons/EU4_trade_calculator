## Verified facts from response_1 (ground truth; do not contradict, do not re-derive)

- V1. Corpus: 82 saves, 3,420,320 `(country,node)` `max_demand` values; `max_demand` exists for every country at every node. `val = max_pow * max_demand`.
- V2. For a country without `trade_embargoed_by`, outside its home node, its top-province nodes and its away-collecting nodes, `max_demand` is the same number at every node: 42,632 of 42,636 (save,country) groups (99.99%) have exactly one value (the 4 others: SUN and DLI in S80/U01). So no node property (goods, node size, trade company region) is involved for ordinary countries.
- V3. Classes: *domestic* = the node of `country.trade_port` OR a node whose `top_provinces[0]` is the tag; everything else is *foreign*. Top-node value equals home-node value in 839 of 873 cases (96%); differs from both in 16 (colonial nations C05/C08/C10 and BNG in each of the four played-save files; these are the home-bonus cases of V8). Home vs foreign value (non-embargoed, 42,636 groups): home lower 20,233, equal 20,569, higher 1,834; ratio home/foreign min 0.528, median 1.0, max 4.253.
- V4. The foreign value is one scalar per country-save with 749 distinct values. Most common: 1.009 (13,786 country-saves), 1.036, 0.993, 1.007, 1.031, 1.339, 1.026, 1.012 (these 8 = 59.7%). Min 0.281 (ZUN, S42), max 2.166. Below 1.0 in 3,815 country-saves (207 tags). Exactly 1.000: once (S28 ETH). Correlation with prestige -0.09, power projection 0.17, mercantilism 0.09, government rank 0.08 (R² <= 0.03): no single one of these four explains it.
- V5. TUR (S79, 1665.4.22): foreign 2.110, domestic 1.895 (clean rows with no embargoer own power). GEN (S79): foreign 1.907, no clean domestic row (genua 1.241 is embargoed by SWI, PAP, LAN).
- V6. Embargo acts inside `max_demand`: embargoed-country rows (9,324): no embargoer with own power -> reduced 2 / not reduced 7,968; some embargoer with own power -> reduced 1,206 / not reduced 148 (reduced = more than 0.3% below the class cap; 1,284 / 70 with an absolute 0.0015 threshold) (120 of the 148 have embargoer share < 1% of the node's own power). *Own power* = `max_pow - prev` (province + ship + flat merchant/capital extras, NOT propagated). With province+ship only, 320 reduced rows have no embargoer power: the embargoer's flat merchant power counts.
- V7. Steering and merchant-without-action do not change `max_demand`; collecting away halves it (R02).
- V8. Home-node bonus: in S79, S80, U01, U02 (2 saves; S79 = U02 and S80 = U01 are copies, independence of S79 and S80 not established), for non-embargoed countries with >= 1 steering merchant and nobody collecting away, home - top = exactly 0.1 x number of steering merchants (16 of 16; e.g. C05 8 merchants +0.8, BNG +0.3/+0.4); with a merchant collecting away home == top (12 of 12). In the 78 start snapshots 831 such countries show home == top (no bonus).
- V9. Inside one campaign (TUR, saves U03 1691.1.9, U04 1691.11.1, U05 1693.4.15, after S79 1665.4.22 and S80 1682.4.18; the saves are not independent): domestic class (embargo-clean nodes hormuz, basra, philippines) 2.216 / 2.216 / 2.214 (alexandria is 2.190 / 2.190 / 2.188: HUN has own power 7.0 there); foreign class (crimea, gulf_of_aden) 1.932 / 1.925 / 2.289 (crimea in U05); no embargoer of TUR has own power at these nodes. So domestic minus foreign flips sign within one country (S79: foreign higher; S80, U03, U04: domestic higher; U05: foreign higher; the foreign value is one scalar shared by 36-44 embargo-clean foreign nodes per save). The foreign scalar rose by 19% between U04 and U05 while TUR's ideas, reforms, technology levels, mercantilism (30) and estate loyalty tiers were unchanged; its modifier list changed (`trade_success` expired, `discontent_sowed` and `dip_boost` added).
- V10. The home bonus of V8 is also present in U03, U04 (dated 1691.11.1, a tick day) and U05: BNG home - top = 0.400 (4 steering merchants, nobody collecting away); BRZ, C02, SPA, TUR (a merchant collecting away) 0.000 in all three saves (12 of 12 country-saves); C05 (8 steering merchants, 0.800), C08 (3, 0.300) and C10 (7, 0.700; 6 and 0.600 in S79/S80) fit in all three saves as before, so four tags, one campaign. The bonus is therefore not a mid-month artefact; TUR never qualifies for the bonus test in these saves.
- V11. U06 (TUR, 1696.3.25; after U05 1693.4.15; same campaign): adm and dip technology 22 -> 23, parliament issue changed, 9th merchant; ideas, reforms, policies, modifier list and mercantilism unchanged. TUR `max_demand`: embargo-free domestic nodes (home, hormuz, basra) 2.214 -> 2.196 (x0.9919); embargo-free foreign node crimea 2.289 -> 2.351 (x1.0271); genua (embargoer SPA) 2.125 -> 2.180; `philippines` 2.214 -> 2.078 in the save where embargoer C12 first has own power there (17.0); `venice` passive 2.203 -> collecting away 1.094 (x0.4966). The technology step did not raise the scalar; prestige, power projection, mercantilism, government rank, absolutism, legitimacy, tradition and inflation do not track the six TUR values (1.895, 2.039, 2.216, 2.216, 2.214, 2.196). (response_1, 'Update 2026-10-05 - save U06', U-3)

- V12. (Venice series U07-U30, new game VEN 1444.11.11 to 1445.7.2, 24 saves; `max_demand` of all 53,290 country-node values.) Within a game `max_demand` changes only when a 1st of the month lies between two saves (15 of 15 pairs without a 1st: 0 changes; 8 of 8 pairs with a 1st: 3,843-22,369 changes). A player action of the month (recall, embargo) shows at the next 1st.
- V13. VEN home-node `max_demand` = base + `transfer_home_bonus`; the country field = 0.1 x number of steering merchants (0, 1, 2, 3 merchants: 0.0, 0.1, 0.2, 0.3) and follows the merchant change at once; `max_demand` follows at the next 1st (1.023, 1.122, 1.222, 1.322 on 4.1, 5.1, 6.1, 7.2); the foreign class of VEN does not depend on the merchant count (0.946, 0.945). VEN never collects away and is never embargoed. A start snapshot (U07, S01) has no bonus because no 1st has passed. Population rule "field = 0.1 x steering merchant entries" fits 7,355 of 7,574 country-saves with a steering merchant.
- V14. (Mechanism refuted by V17; the observation stays.) Between two fresh games of the same scenario (S01 and U07, VEN 1444.11.11) 535 of 666 countries have identical `max_demand` everywhere; 129 have a different random ruler DIP; of these 34 (two-class countries: a home value and one foreign value) differ in the foreign-class value by exactly 0.005 x delta-DIP (34 of 34), 88 (one-class: same value at all nodes) do not change, 7 two-class countries with values {1.009, 1.036} do not change. In U07, 238 of 281 two-class countries satisfy foreign - domestic = 0.012 + 0.005 x ruler DIP; the formula does not hold in later-era saves (S67 19 of 285; U06 1 of 30).
- V15. First 1st of a fresh game (1444.11.30 -> 12.1): the foreign-class value of GEN and NOV rises by 0.202, of HSA by 0.102, RAG and PSK by 0.100 (republics with `merchants_reform` / `veche_republic`); the other 26 republics by at most 0.002 and VEN by 0.000; 236 of 666 countries change, 224 upward (median +0.002). At later 1sts 47-280 countries change by about +-0.002 (median), no country-block field tracks the change (best |r| 0.83 for `corruption` on 31 countries, not reproduced on the other ticks).
- V17. (Controlled experiments 2026-10-07, response_1 'Update 2026-10-07', single changes on VEN from one base save; VEN fields identical in the four controls.) Changing VEN's ruler DIP 4 -> 6 in a running game changes no `max_demand` (E02): the "+0.005 per DIP point" of V14 is not a mechanism; the cross-game observation of V14 stands with its cause open.
- V18. +10 % `global_trade_power` / `global_own_trade_power` / `global_foreign_trade_power` add +0.100 to `max_demand` at all nodes / at the home node only / at every other node (E03, E04, E05): additive, domestic = home node, foreign = the rest (for VEN; top-province nodes not examined). dip and adm tech 3 -> 4: no change (E10, E11). Completing `trade_ideas`: +0.200 everywhere (E22).
- V19. Home bonus: 0 as soon as one merchant collects away (P03: 0.200 -> 0.000, venice 1.213 -> 1.013, although wien still steers); unchanged when the ragusa steerer steers away from home (P01); a fourth steerer at constantinople (no link into the home node) adds nothing (P04). Inferred rule: steering merchants in nodes with a direct link into the home node count, whatever link they steer.
- V16. AI embargoes in that game: GEN embargoes LAN (field on 1445.1.1), LAN embargoes GEN (by 1.15), FRA embargoes ENG (by 3.31); each lowers `max_demand` of the embargoed country only from the next 1st (LAN at crimea 1.057 -> 0.953 on 2.1; ENG at champagne 1.073 -> 0.899 on 4.1).
