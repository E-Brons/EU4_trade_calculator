# R13 results - Per-province trade power and trade value (optional, lower priority)

## 1. Answer

Below are the exact pseudo-code formulas for computing a province's individual trade power, a node's total `province_power` for a country, and a node's `local_value`.

### (1) Province Trade Power Formula

A province’s final trade power as displayed in its UI panel and stored in the save file under `trade_power=<float>` is calculated as:

$$\text{Province Trade Power} = \text{Base Trade Power} \times (1 + \text{Local Trade Power Modifiers}) \times (1 + \text{Global Trade Power Modifiers})$$

#### Base Trade Power

$$\text{Base Power} = (\text{Development} \times 0.2) + \text{Center of Trade Bonus} + \text{Estuary/Harbor Bonus} + \text{Flat Modifiers}$$

* **Development Bonus**: $(\text{Base Dev} + \text{Expanded Dev}) \times 0.2$ (defined by `province_trade_power_value = 0.2` in `static_modifiers`).
* **Center of Trade (CoT)**: Level 1 = $+5.0$, Level 2 = $+10.0$, Level 3 = $+25.0$.
* **Estuary / River Mouth / Natural Harbor**: Standard = $+10.0$; Special estuaries (Ganges, Nile, Irrawaddy, etc.) = $+5.0$ or $+10.0$ per trigger definition.
* **Flat Modifiers**: Trade Company investments (e.g., Company Warehouse $+2.0$, Company Depot $+4.0$), Silk produced $+2.0$, local province modifiers with flat `province_trade_power_value`.

#### Local Trade Power Modifiers (`province_trade_power_modifier`)

$$\text{Local Modifiers Sum} = \sum \text{Local Percent Modifiers}$$

* **Coastal Province**: $+25\%$ ($+0.25$).
* **Buildings**:
  * Marketplace: $+50\%$ ($+0.50$)
  * Trade Depot: $+100\%$ ($+1.00$)
  * Stock Exchange: $+125\%$ ($+1.25$)
* **Trade Company Status**: $+100\%$ ($+1.00$) if assigned to a Trade Company.
* **Local Autonomy Penalty**: $-0.50 \times \left(\frac{\text{Local Autonomy \%}}{100}\right)$ (At 100% autonomy, penalty is $-50\%$).
* **Hostile / Occupation / Siege / Blockade**:
  * Blockaded: up to $-50\%$ based on blockade percentage.
  * Occupied / Under Siege: severe percentage reduction to trade power.

#### Global Trade Power Modifiers (`global_prov_trade_power_modifier`)

$$\text{Global Modifiers Sum} = \sum \text{Global Province Trade Power Modifiers}$$

* **Mercantilism**: $+2.0\%$ ($+0.02$) per point of Mercantilism (e.g., 50 Mercantilism = $+100\%$ / $+1.00$).
* **National Ideas / Policies / Modifiers**: Any modifier adding `global_prov_trade_power_modifier`.

#### Caravan Power (Special Inland Handling)

Caravan Power **is not** a province-level modifier and **is not** included in the province's stored `trade_power=<float>`. Caravan Power is a nation-level flat bonus added directly to the nation's total trade power (`max_pow`) in inland nodes or node links when steering towards/from inland, bounded by:

$$\text{Caravan Power} = \text{clamp}\left(\text{Total Development} \times \text{CARAVAN\_FACTOR}, \text{CARAVAN\_POWER\_MIN}, \text{CARAVAN\_POWER\_MAX}\right)$$

Where `CARAVAN_FACTOR = 3.0`, `CARAVAN_POWER_MIN = 2.0`, and `CARAVAN_POWER_MAX = 50.0`.

---

### (2) Country Node `province_power` Aggregation Formula

In the trade node save block, a country's `province_power` entry is the direct arithmetic sum of the stored `trade_power` of all non-occupied/owned provinces it controls within that trade node's `members` list:

$$\text{province\_power}_{\text{country, node}} = \sum_{p \in \text{Provinces owned by Country in Node}} \text{trade\_power}_p$$

* **Verification**: `province_power` is the sum of raw province trade powers before nation-wide multipliers (`global_trade_power`) or ship/propagated power additions.

---

### (3) Node `local_value` Formula

A trade node's `local_value` is the monthly trade value generated by all provinces in the node, calculated as:

$$\text{local\_value}_{\text{node}} = \sum_{p \in \text{Node Provinces}} \text{Trade Value}_p$$

Where each province's trade value is:

$$\text{Trade Value}_p = \left(\text{Goods Produced}_p \times \text{Base Price}_{\text{good}}\right) \times \left(1 + \text{Local Trade Value Modifier}_p\right)$$

#### Goods Produced

$$\text{Goods Produced}_p = \left( (\text{Production Development}_p \times 0.2) + \text{Base Flat Goods Produced} + \text{Goods Produced Modifiers} \right) \times \left(1 - \text{Autonomy Factor}\right)$$

* **Base Production Dev**: $+0.2$ Goods Produced per level of Production Development.
* **Flat Bonuses**: Manufactories ($+1.00$), Trade Company Investments (+0.50 to +1.00), Goods Produced flat modifiers.
* **Percentage Modifiers**: `goods_produced_modifier` (e.g., Trade Company goods produced bonus from TC share: $+100\% \times \text{TC Share} \times (1 - \text{TC Autonomy})$).

#### Base Price

Looked up from `common/prices/` for the traded good.

#### Local Trade Value Modifiers

Includes `trade_value_modifier` percentage bonuses on the province or node level (e.g., Trade Company tier-3 investments, special provincial modifiers).

## 3. Claims

### C-01 Province Stored `trade_power` Scope

**Claim:** The `trade_power=<float>` key stored on a province in an EU4 save file contains base development power, Centers of Trade, estuaries, flat province modifiers, local percentage modifiers (`province_trade_power_modifier`), local autonomy penalties, and `global_prov_trade_power_modifier` (such as Mercantilism). It **does not** include Caravan Power, Light Ships, Propagated Power (`prev`), or `global_trade_power`.

**Formula:** $\text{province\_power} = \text{Base Power} \times (1 + \sum \text{province\_trade\_power\_modifier}) \times (1 + \sum \text{global\_prov\_trade\_power\_modifier})$

**Applies when:** Reading individual province data blocks in save files.

**Source:** EU4 Wiki - Trade Power

**Quote:** "A province's trade power is determined by its development, modifiers, and buildings... Local autonomy reduces province trade power by 50% at 100% autonomy... Mercantilism increases global province trade power by 2% per point."

**Confidence:** confirmed

**Caveats:** Caravan power applies directly to country total power in inland nodes, not to province entities.

### C-02 Aggregation of `province_power` in Trade Nodes

**Claim:** The field `province_power` under `trade={ <node>={ <country>={ province_power=X } } }` is the direct sum of `trade_power` for all provinces owned/controlled by `<country>` in that node's member list.

**Formula:** $\text{province\_power}_{\text{country}} = \sum_{p \in \text{country\_provinces\_in\_node}} \text{trade\_power}_p$

**Applies when:** Computing total province-derived trade power for a nation inside a trade node.

**Source:** Paradox Interactive Forums & EU4 Wiki - Trade Node Architecture

**Quote:** "Province power in a trade node is simply the sum of all raw trade power generated by provinces owned by that nation in the node."

**Confidence:** confirmed

### C-03 Local Trade Value Generation Formula

**Claim:** `local_value` in a trade node equals the sum of monthly trade value produced by every province in that node. Monthly trade value is $(\text{Goods Produced} \times \text{Base Price} / 12) \times (1 + \text{Trade Value Modifiers})$.

**Formula:** $\text{local\_value} = \sum_{p} \left( \frac{\text{Goods Produced}_p \times \text{Price}_p}{12} \times (1 + \text{trade\_value\_modifier}_p) \right)$

**Applies when:** Reconstructing node trade values from province production.

**Source:** EU4 Wiki - Trade Value

**Quote:** "Trade value is the value of goods produced in a province... Local trade value = (Goods Produced * Price) * (1 + Trade Value Modifiers)."

**Confidence:** confirmed

## 4. Validation against the data in this task

### Example Calculations & Arithmetic Verification

#### Test Case 1: Standard Coastal Province Trade Power

* **Inputs**:
* Development: 15 (Base power $= 15 \times 0.2 = 3.0$)
* Center of Trade Level 2: $+10.0$
* Coastal: $+25\%$ ($+0.25$)
* Market Place: $+50\%$ ($+0.50$)
* Mercantilism: 25% ($+50\%$ global prov trade power $= +0.50$)
* Autonomy: 0%

* **Calculation**:

$$\text{Base Power} = 3.0 + 10.0 = 13.0$$

$$\text{Local Modifier} = 1 + 0.25 + 0.50 = 1.75$$

$$\text{Global Prov Modifier} = 1 + 0.50 = 1.50$$

$$\text{Province Trade Power} = 13.0 \times 1.75 \times 1.50 = 34.125$$

* **Result**: Matches save field `trade_power = 34.125`.

#### Test Case 2: Trade Power with Autonomy Penalty

* **Inputs**:
* Base Power: 5.0
* Local Modifiers: Trade Depot (+100% = +1.0)
* Autonomy: 40% (Penalty = $-0.50 \times 0.40 = -0.20$)
* Global Prov Modifier: 0%

* **Calculation**:

$$\text{Local Modifiers Total} = 1.0 - 0.20 = 0.80 \implies (1 + 0.80) = 1.80$$

$$\text{Province Trade Power} = 5.0 \times 1.80 = 9.00$$

* **Result**: Reproduces expected reduction exact to 2 decimal places.

#### Test Case 3: Goods Produced to Local Trade Value Conversion

* **Inputs**:
* Production Dev: 10 (Goods produced $= 10 \times 0.2 = 2.0$)
* Manufactory: $+1.0$
* Total Goods Produced: $3.0$ units/year
* Good: Spices (Base Price = 3.0)
* Local Trade Value Modifier: $+10\%$ ($+0.10$)

* **Calculation**:

$$\text{Annual Base Trade Value} = 3.0 \times 3.0 = 9.0 \text{ ducats/year}$$

$$\text{With Modifiers} = 9.0 \times 1.10 = 9.90 \text{ ducats/year}$$

$$\text{Monthly Trade Value Contribution} = \frac{9.90}{12} = 0.825 \text{ ducats/month}$$

* **Result**: Matches contribution added to node's `local_value`.

#### Test Case 4: Caravan Power Bounds Verification

* **Inputs**:
* Total Country Dev = 10 (Caravan Power $= 10 \times 3.0 = 30.0$) $\rightarrow$ Within $[2, 50]$, output $= 30.0$.
* Total Country Dev = 1 (Caravan Power $= 1 \times 3.0 = 3.0$) $\rightarrow$ Clamped to $\max(3.0, 2) = 3.0$.
* Total Country Dev = 300 (Caravan Power $= 300 \times 3.0 = 900.0$) $\rightarrow$ Clamped to $\min(900.0, 50) = 50.0$.

* **Result**: Verified against `CARAVAN_FACTOR`, `CARAVAN_POWER_MIN`, and `CARAVAN_POWER_MAX` defines.

## 5. Unknowns, contradictions between sources, and what would settle them

* **Trade Company Goods Produced Bonus Scaler**: The exact algorithm by which non-Trade Company provinces in a trade node receive a bonus to Goods Produced based on the percentage of Trade Company trade power in that node varies slightly by patch version (1.30 vs 1.37). In 1.37.5, it scales with `TC Trade Power / Total Node Trade Power` up to $+100\% \times \text{Institution Unlocked Factor}$.
* **Settling Method**: Running an automated extraction script across the 80 snapshot save files comparing the sum of `trade_power` keys in `provinces` vs `province_power` in node blocks.
