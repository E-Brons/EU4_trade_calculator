## Verified facts from response_1 (ground truth; do not contradict, build on them)

- Only the played TUR campaign saves (S79 1665.4.22 and S80 1682.4.18; U02/U01 are identical copies) carry ship data: 216 node entries with `light_ship`; the 78 start saves have none.
- Assignment storage: `countries.<TAG>.navy[].mission.protect_mission.node` is a 1-based index into the save's node list; keys of the mission block: `retreat_port was_safe_retreat_port node trade current_route_target current_cycle_begin on_my_way`. With it TUR's six node rows are reproduced exactly: Basra Trade Fleet (node 30 malacca, 20 early_frigate + 4 frigate, base 74, entry `light_ship 24, ship_power 74`), Zeila Donanmasi (35 comorin_cape, 13 frigate, 45.5), Suhar Donanmasi (43 basra, 4 frigate, 14), Sinop Donanmasi (49 crimea, 1 early_frigate, 3), Venice Trade Fleet (78 genua, 13 early_frigate + 7 frigate, 63.5), Tirhala Donanmasi (79 venice, 31 early_frigate + 5 frigate, 110.5). Not counted in any entry: Kizildeniz Donanmasi (35 comorin_cape, 1 early_frigate) and Karadeniz Donanmasi (39 gulf_of_aden, 1 frigate; TUR steers there). TUR: 66 early_frigate + 34 frigate = 100 ships on missions, `num_ships_protecting_trade = 98`.
- Base trade power per ship (project file `data/game/light_ships.json`, from `common/units/*.txt`): barque 2.0, caravel 2.5, early_frigate 3.0, frigate 3.5, heavy_frigate 4.0, great_frigate 5.0. Other ship types inside a protect fleet add nothing (S79 BLG venice: 3 frigate + 3 galiot gives `light_ship 3, ship_power 10.5`; S80 YUE canton: 2 early_frigate + 3 galleon + 9 galleass gives `light_ship 2, ship_power 6.0`).
- `ship_power = f x sum(base trade_power of the counted light ships)`, f multiplicative over all types in the entry, f in {1.0, 1.05, 1.1, 1.2} in 216 entries (1.0: 204; 1.1: 9; 1.2: 2; 1.05: 1), never below 1.
- There is no per-node ship cap near 24 or 36 (largest: S80 TUR the_moluccas 65 ships, comorin_cape 60, S79 SPA sevilla 42); no save has ships at an inland node; counted ships have `morale` 2.08..5.729 and no reduction is visible.

- S79/S80 SAM `polynesia_node`: 35.4 = 29.5 x 1.2.
Country-independent facts: DAN has f = 1.0 at `north_sea` and `st_lawrence` but 1.1 at `lubeck`; SPA has 1.0 at `malacca`, `genua`, `sevilla` and 1.05 at `ivory_coast`; 204 entries have f = 1.0 (including 62 home collectors in S79 and 51 in S80).
