## Verified facts from response_1 (ground truth; do not contradict, build on them)

- Assignment storage: `countries.<TAG>.navy[].mission.protect_mission.node` is a 1-based index into the save's node list; keys of the mission block: `retreat_port was_safe_retreat_port node trade current_route_target current_cycle_begin on_my_way`. With it TUR's six node rows are reproduced exactly: Basra Trade Fleet (node 30 malacca, 20 early_frigate + 4 frigate, base 74, entry `light_ship 24, ship_power 74`), Zeila Donanmasi (35 comorin_cape, 13 frigate, 45.5), Suhar Donanmasi (43 basra, 4 frigate, 14), Sinop Donanmasi (49 crimea, 1 early_frigate, 3), Venice Trade Fleet (78 genua, 13 early_frigate + 7 frigate, 63.5), Tirhala Donanmasi (79 venice, 31 early_frigate + 5 frigate, 110.5). Not counted in any entry: Kizildeniz Donanmasi (35 comorin_cape, 1 early_frigate) and Karadeniz Donanmasi (39 gulf_of_aden, 1 frigate; TUR steers there). TUR: 66 early_frigate + 34 frigate = 100 ships on missions, `num_ships_protecting_trade = 98`.
- Base trade power per ship (project file `data/game/light_ships.json`, from `common/units/*.txt`): barque 2.0, caravel 2.5, early_frigate 3.0, frigate 3.5, heavy_frigate 4.0, great_frigate 5.0. Other ship types inside a protect fleet add nothing (S79 BLG venice: 3 frigate + 3 galiot gives `light_ship 3, ship_power 10.5`; S80 YUE canton: 2 early_frigate + 3 galleon + 9 galleass gives `light_ship 2, ship_power 6.0`).
- `ship_power = f x sum(base trade_power of the counted light ships)`, f multiplicative over all types in the entry, f in {1.0, 1.05, 1.1, 1.2}, never below 1: 539 entries of S79, S80, U03, U04, U05 (1.0: 524; 1.1: 12; 1.2: 2; 1.05: 1). U03 (1691.1.9), U04 (1691.11.1) and U05 (1693.4.15) are further saves of the same TUR campaign (323 more entries).
- f != 1 has two data-established sources (response_1, section 'Update 2026-10-05'): (1) leaderless fleets: the country's national idea group - `fijian_ideas` (LAI, VIL, VNL) 1.1, `luzon_ideas` (MNA, TDO) 1.1, `samoan_ideas` (SAM) 1.2; these groups occur in no pair with f = 1 and every other national set has f = 1, 0 exceptions in 332 leaderless (save, country) pairs; f is constant per country; 'lucky nations' (`luck=yes`) is rejected (30 lucky pairs, all f = 1). (2) fleets with a `leader`: 5 entries, all f != 1; `f = 1 + 0.05 x maneuver` fits the 3 distinct leaders (maneuver 2: DAN lubeck 1.10, HOL english_channel 1.10 in three saves; maneuver 1: SPA ivory_coast 1.05); fire, siege and shock do not fit. No entry has a leader and one of the three idea groups together.
- `ship_power` is fully inside `max_pow` (coefficient exactly 1): 476 of 476 entries with ships; in the 19 entry pairs where only ships changed, `d(max_pow) = d(ship_power)` and `d(val) = d(max_pow x max_demand)` in 19 of 19, also at a passive node (TUR hormuz, 0 to 11 ships, val 463.095 to 548.411). A node's `pull_power`/`retain_power` changes by exactly the country's change in effective power. Income from a ship therefore arises indirectly, at the country's collecting nodes downstream; a replay estimate (not a recorded value) is +0.1 to +0.4 ducats per month per frigate at a collecting node, 0 where no collector of the country lies downstream.
- There is no per-node ship cap near 24 or 36 (largest: S80 TUR the_moluccas 65 ships, comorin_cape 60, S79 SPA sevilla 42); no save has ships at an inland node; counted ships have `morale` 2.08..5.729 and no reduction is visible.
