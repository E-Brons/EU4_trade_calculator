## Verified facts from response_1 (ground truth from 82 saves; do not contradict, build on them)

- `local_value` (ducats per month) `= sum over 33 goods of trade_goods_size[i] * current_price[i] / 12`, with `current_price` from the save's own top-level `change_price` block (key order of that block = index order of `trade_goods_size`: nogoods, grain, wine, wool, cloth, fish, fur, salt, naval_supplies, copper, gold, iron, slaves, ivory, tea, chinaware, spices, coffee, cotton, sugar, tobacco, cocoa, silk, dyes, tropical_wood, livestock, incense, glass, paper, gems, coal, cloves, unknown). Reproduced to 0.002 ducats in 5,748 of 6,502 nodes; the other 754 are 742 nodes containing incense plus 12 played-save nodes (`hormuz`, `white_sea`, `genua`).
- Incense: in the start saves, 702 of 746 nodes with incense fit `local_value` to 0.002 when incense is valued at `1.1 x current_price` (a 10% premium: extra 0.25 per unit x 12 where the save price is 2.5, 0.2125 at 2.125, 0.30 at 3.0; the save's `current_price` for incense is 2.5 in 40 saves, 2.125 in 26, 3.0 in 16). The other 44 nodes are all `australia` (0.6 incense), where the plain `current_price` fits. S01 `gulf_of_aden`: stored 4.952 vs 4.848 with the plain price 2.5; `australia` S01: stored 1.375, plain price gives 1.375, premium gives 1.387.
- `trade_goods_size[i]` is about `0.2 x sum(base_production of provinces with that trade good in the node)` in the 1444 saves (median ratio to `0.2 x base_production` is 1.00 for 29 of 30 goods in S01, S14 and S36; cloves 1.1, i.e. 0.22). In the later saves S42 and S60 most goods have a median ratio of 1.02-1.2, so the plain 0.2 rule does not hold there.
- `province_power(C, N)` = sum of stored province `trade_power` over provinces with `trade=N` and `controller=C` (320 of 53,944 entry-node pairs fail: 310 in the played saves, 10 in start saves). Provinces held by rebels (`controller=REB`) count for no entry but are in the node's `p_pow` and `total`. Of 792 occupied provinces, the controller's entry includes the province in 658 (start saves: 580 of 580).
- Province `trade_power = (0.2 x development + centre_of_trade_flat) x M x (1 - 0.005 x local_autonomy)`, `development = base_tax + base_production + base_manpower`, flat 5 / 10 / 25 for `center_of_trade` 1 / 2 / 3; `M` is not stored per province and is additive. Rows (S01 unless noted): `-2058 Mewar` dev 9, no CoT, autonomy 0, M 1.2: 0.2 x 9 x 1.2 = 2.16, stored 2.16. `-518 Chittor` dev 5, CoT 1: (1.0 + 5) x 1.2 = 7.2, stored 7.2. `-2087 Ajmer` dev 8, CoT 1, autonomy 1.0: (1.6 + 5) x 1.2 x 0.995 = 7.8804, stored 7.88. `-2067 Gorwar` dev 9, autonomy 2.0: 1.8 x 1.2 x 0.99 = 2.1384, stored 2.138. `-1 Stockholm` dev 14, CoT 2, M 1.45: (2.8 + 10) x 1.45 = 18.56, stored 18.56. `-23 Bergenhus` dev 7, CoT 2, M 1.45: (1.4 + 10) x 1.45 = 16.53, stored 16.53. `-568 Chittagong` dev 13, CoT 3, autonomy 6, M 1.45: (2.6 + 25) x 1.45 x 0.97 = 38.819, stored 38.805. `-4457 Khambhat` dev 11, CoT 3, autonomy 5, M 1.45: (2.2 + 25) x 1.45 x 0.975 = 38.454, stored 38.433 (the CoT-3 rows deviate by 0.04-0.05%).
- Distribution of `M` in S01 (2,469 provinces with positive base and controller = owner, rounded to 0.1): 1.2: 1,443; 1.4 (= 1.45): 591; 1.3: 137; 1.5: 122; 1.7: 34; 1.8: 29; 1.6: 23. Provinces at M = 1.45 lie mostly in coastal nodes (nippon 52, malacca 42, lubeck 29, north_sea 29, gulf_of_aden 29, mexico 28), provinces at M = 1.2 mostly in inland nodes (samarkand 50, kiev 46, timbuktu 43, yumen 41, ethiopia 40); no coastal flag exists in the province block. Buildings in the 1444 saves are almost only `fort_15th`.

- Update 2026-10-05, Venice series U07-U30: `province_power` of every entry equals the sum of the stored `trade_power` of its controlled provinces in 100 % of the entries in each save written on a 1st (773/773 or 772/772 in U10, U14, U20, U25, U27, U28, U29) and in the bookmark save U07; in the other saves it holds for 63-98 % of entries, because the provinces' `trade_power` moves between 1sts while node entries stay frozen (`response_1`, 'Update 2026-10-05 - Venice series'). The earlier inference about mid-month staleness is confirmed. Do not re-ask it.
